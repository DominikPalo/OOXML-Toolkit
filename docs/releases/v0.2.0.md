**OOXML Toolkit 0.2.0** adds previews for **EMF and WMF** pictures — the Windows metafile formats that Office uses for pasted CAD drawings, Visio diagrams and Excel charts, and which no browser engine can display.

![Animated tour](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DominikPalo/OOXML-Toolkit/main/docs/demo.gif)

## What's new

- **EMF, EMF+ and WMF preview.** Metafile images used to show “This image format cannot be previewed”. They are now converted to SVG and shown in the image view (**Preview** tab), in the **slide preview** and in the **comparison** view.
- **Handles huge drawings.** Large CAD floor plans with hundreds of thousands of shapes (a real 25 MB export has 410 000) render correctly. This needed `emf-converter` 4.11.3, which fixes a call-stack overflow upstream; the local workaround patch is gone along with `patch-package`.

Good to know: a metafile is sized to the content that is actually drawn (not to its header frame as PowerPoint does), text uses the fonts installed on your machine, and a very large file takes a moment to convert (about 2 seconds for the 25 MB example).

### First launch — the builds are not notarized yet

- **macOS:** drag the app from the `.dmg` to *Applications* and open it once — macOS blocks it. Then open
  **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to
  “OOXML Toolkit was blocked”. (macOS 14 and earlier: right-click the app → **Open** instead.)
  From a terminal, this does the same: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/OOXML Toolkit.app"`.
  Notarization needs a paid Apple Developer ID; until then this one-time step is required.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app — choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** `chmod +x OOXML-Toolkit-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage && ./OOXML-Toolkit-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`.
  If it does not start, install FUSE 2 (`libfuse2` / `fuse2`).

## About OOXML Toolkit

View, edit and compare Office Open XML packages (`.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx` and friends) right down to the XML: a tree of every part and element, source editor and inspector, worksheet / document / slide previews, text / regex / XPath search, side-by-side comparison, bookmarks and history, and a package check. See the [README](https://github.com/DominikPalo/OOXML-Toolkit#readme) and the [user guide](https://github.com/DominikPalo/OOXML-Toolkit/blob/main/docs/guide.md).

## Known limitations

- Password-protected Office files and legacy binary formats (`.doc`, `.xls`, `.ppt`) cannot be opened.
- The package check verifies structure, not ECMA-376 schema validity.
- The slide and worksheet previews are simplified (no fonts/fills/charts; raw cell values).
- Builds are not notarized (see above) and there is no auto-update yet.
